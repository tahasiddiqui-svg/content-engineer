Build log

Every reviewer correction gets logged. Seen twice, it becomes a rule the pipeline enforces, so the same mistake never reaches a reviewer again. Real entries, lightly redacted.

6 Oct 2026 · rule L3

A score never proves quality

The copy scorer gave an invite 94 out of 100. Read as a human, it never said where or what the event was.

rule: the word by word read outranks every score
5 Oct 2026 · rule L1

Time words are read when the asset is consumed

A pre event video said "tonight". It would be watched days earlier.

rule: check every relative date against the send date
5 Oct 2026 · rule L1

Never invent social proof

A draft invite said "several guests asked". No guest had. I caught it in the read.

rule: every claim traces to a named, dated source
3 Oct 2026 · rule L1

Test what a number counts

A draft showed event capacity in a line the reader would take as invitations sent.

rule: name what every number counts before it ships
22 Sep 2026 · rule L4

One flag means audit the whole asset

A reviewer flagged one blunt verb. The fix had to cover every word of its kind, not that one line.

rule: sweep the full asset for the class, report every fix
